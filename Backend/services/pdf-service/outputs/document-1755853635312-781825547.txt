ABBREVIATIONAL HIGHLIGHTS:  
   
• Maximum thrice in a month with 15 minutes grace on schedule shift timing will be tolerated. 
• If any of the employee will present for consecutive 3 months without any leaves and without being late will be rewarded 
by additional one day reimbursement in fourth month 
• The company recognizes that employee may have to work on a holiday or a weekly off due to business exigencies. The 
company may or may not compensate an employee. 
• Minimum working duration in a day should be 05 hours. 
• First Half Day can be avail from 09:15 AM to 02:15 PM 
• Second Half Day can be avail from 01:45 PM  
• If the approval was secure for half day but avail the full day leave will count unapproved. 
• Employees have to punch every time while they are coming inside the office or going outside the office whether for any 
call or buying something official (Does not apply during pandemic situation).  
• Desk break can be taken itself at your work stations.  
• Lunch Break --- 01:45 PM to 02:15 PM (You need to be on your respective work stations anyhow at 02:13 not after that) 
• If you want to share any edible item you can distribute during lunch hour (01:45 – 02:15) or tea break (04:30 – 04:40 PM). 
• Beverages mean tea or coffee. 
• It is advisable to bring your own water bottles and fill it on your break timings only. 
• To foster a productive and efficient work environment, employees are encouraged to complete their full 09-hour shift 
while minimizing extended breaks and non-work-related activities. Maintaining focus during working hours helps ensure 
smooth operations and enhances overall team performance.  
 
 
We appreciate your commitment and cooperation in creating a positive and efficient workplace.
