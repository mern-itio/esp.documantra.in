Purpose
• This Code of Conduct serves as a guiding framework for maintaining professionalism, ethical integrity, and
workplace discipline. Every employee is encouraged to uphold these principles to foster a respectful, safe,
and productive work environment.
1. Workplace Behaviour & Ethics
• Respect & Professionalism: Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and stakeholders with
courtesy, dignity, and professionalism. Any form of discrimination, harassment, or inappropriate behavior
is strictly prohibited.
• Integrity & Honesty: Employees must demonstrate honesty and integrity in all professional dealings,
avoiding any form of unethical or fraudulent conduct.
• Confidentiality & Data Protection: Employees are responsible for safeguarding sensitive company and
client information. This includes, but is not limited to, business operations, client data, personal employee
information, salary details, and any specific assignments or directives from management. Unauthorized
disclosure or misuse of such information is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.
• Compliance with Policies & Laws: Employees are required to adhere to all company policies, industry
regulations, and applicable legal requirements in the course of their duties.
2. Workplace Conduct & Responsibilities
• Attendance & Punctuality: Employees are encouraged to maintain regular attendance and adhere to
designated working hours to ensure a smooth workflow. Persistent absenteeism or tardiness may
necessitate corrective action.
• Dress Code: Employees should dress in a manner that aligns with professional standards and company
guidelines.
• Responsible Use of Company Resources: Office assets, including computers, software, email, and the
internet, should be utilized responsibly for work-related purposes. Personal use should be minimal and
within company policy.
• Workplace Safety: Employees are expected to follow safety guidelines and report any potential hazards to
maintain a secure and compliant work environment.

• Harassment & Discrimination: Any form of harassment, bullying, or discrimination based on gender, race,

• Substance Abuse: The use, possession, or distribution of alcohol, drugs, or illegal substances during work

• Conflict of Interest: Employees should avoid situations that may result in a conflict of interest with the

• Unauthorized Disclosure of Information: Sharing confidential company or client information, including

violation and may result in disciplinary or legal consequences.
4. Disciplinary Measures
• Failure to adhere to this Code of Conduct may result in appropriate action, which may include:
• A verbal or written warning
• Suspension or termination of employment
• Legal action, if deemed necessary
• All employees are requested to acknowledge and uphold this policy in their daily professional conduct. By
demonstrating ethical responsibility and professionalism, we contribute to a positive and respectful
workplace for all. For any clarifications, employees are encouraged to reach out to the HR department.
